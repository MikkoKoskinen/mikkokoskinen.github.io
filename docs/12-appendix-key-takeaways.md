# Appendix 2 — Key takeaways

1. **Governance is policy + process + people, not just tooling.** Tools (Agent 365, Defender, Purview, Copilot Studio Kit) enforce what you decide — they cannot substitute for clear policy and accountable owners.
2. **Maker onboarding is the highest-leverage control you have.** One Entra group, one PPAC Authoring Group setting — gates everything downstream with zero manual IT work per maker.
3. **Zone assignment drives every other governance decision.** Default new makers to Zone 1. Promotion to Zone 2/3 should be a deliberate, documented decision — not a default.
4. **MCP and A2A are real attack surfaces today, not future risk.** Treat external MCP servers and A2A connections as untrusted by default. Remember: disabling A2A takes up to 48 hours to fully propagate.
5. **Plan for lifecycle before agents reach critical business use.** Who reacts when something goes wrong? How is an agent retired? Define the answers early — a difficult conversation now is easier than an incident later.

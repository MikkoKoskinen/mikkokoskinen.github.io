# Part B — Before you touch PPAC

Before you configure a single environment group or DLP policy, answer these three sets of questions. They cascade — skip this step and every technical configuration becomes a debate later.

### Maker onboarding
- Who can build — open access or opt-in?
- Who owns the process: IT, CoE, or the business?
- These decisions determine your Entra group design.

### Environment strategy
- Personal sandboxes per maker, or shared environments?
- How many tiers, and who approves promotion to production?
- These decisions determine your Environment Group structure.

### Security & licensing
- Which license path fits your organization?
- Which connectors are allowed per environment type?
- How is message/capacity budgeted?
- These decisions determine your DLP policy design.
